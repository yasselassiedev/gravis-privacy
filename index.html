Privacy Policy

Gravis: Brick Breaker · Developer: Yasselassie · Effective date: November 26, 2026

This policy explains what information the game Gravis: Brick Breaker for Android and iPhone ("the app", "we") handles, why, and the choices you have. The short version: we do not run any servers, we do not ask for your name, email or any account, and your game progress stays on your device. The app shows ads and offers optional in-app purchases through Google and Apple services, which process some data as described below.

1. Information stored on your device

The app saves your game progress locally on your device: best score, gameplay statistics (for example games played and spheres destroyed), unlocked and equipped cosmetics, and settings. This data is not sent to us. It may be included in your device's backup if you have backups enabled (Android backup in your Google account, or iCloud / computer backup on iPhone). Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows banner, interstitial and optional rewarded ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers,
  • your IP address, used to estimate your approximate location,
  • information about your device and the app (such as model, operating system, and crash and performance diagnostics),
  • interactions with ads (such as views and taps).

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown, using Google's User Messaging Platform. You can change your choice at any time from the Privacy options button in the main menu. On any Android device you can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Google → Ads).

On iPhone, the app asks for your permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt before the advertising identifier (IDFA) may be used to track you across apps and websites owned by other companies. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", it is not used for that, and the game plays exactly the same. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. In-app purchases (Google Play / Apple App Store)

Purchases (cosmetics and "Remove Ads") are processed entirely by Google Play on Android and by Apple's App Store on iPhone. We never see your payment details. The app only receives confirmation of which items you own, so it can unlock them and restore them if you reinstall. See the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Play Terms of Service, or, on iPhone, the Apple Privacy Policy.

4. Google Play Games and Apple Game Center (optional)

If Google Play Games is available and you are signed in to it, your high score and achievements are sent to Google Play Games so they can appear on leaderboards and in your Play Games profile, together with your Play Games player name and avatar. This is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. You can manage your Play Games profile and visibility in the Google Play Games settings.

On iPhone, if you are signed in to Apple Game Center, your high score and achievements are sent to Game Center so they can appear on its leaderboard and in your Game Center profile, together with your Game Center nickname. This is handled by Apple under the Apple Privacy Policy. You can manage Game Center in Settings → Game Center.

5. What we do not do

  • We do not require an account or collect your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not operate our own servers or analytics; data processing happens in the Google and Apple services listed above.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help resolve it.

7. Data retention and deletion

Game progress stays on your device until you clear the app's data or uninstall it. Data collected by Google for advertising, purchases or Play Games is retained and deleted according to Google's policies; you can manage it at myaccount.google.com. Data handled by Apple for App Store purchases and Game Center follows Apple's policies; you can manage it at privacy.apple.com.

8. Security

The Google and Apple services used by the app transmit data over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes. The effective date at the top shows the latest version. Significant changes will be reflected in the app's store listing.
